### Independent Builder · AI · Automation · Research

I build small, verifiable systems: research pipelines that cannot fool themselves, automation that leaves an audit
trail, and AI-assisted workflows where every claim is backed by a file you can hash.

**Currently building — ANT-0**, an independent research & development lab for systematic market research.
The lab is private; its methods are not. I publish the architecture, the methodology and the engineering lessons.

- 🧪 **Research discipline** — pre-registration sealed by SHA-256, blind power gates, single-pass readings, a global trial registry.
- ⚙️ **Automation** — scheduled, append-only, replay-verified pipelines that run unattended on a small server.
- 🤖 **AI-assisted engineering** — an AI agent does the heavy lifting; humans keep the governance decisions.

**What you will find here**
- [`ant0-lab`](https://github.com/BayRai/ant0-lab) — public face of the lab: architecture, methodology, build logs and articles.
- [`ant0-lab/build-logs`](https://github.com/BayRai/ant0-lab/tree/main/build-logs) — a weekly, honest log of what was built, broken and fixed.
- [`ant0-lab/articles`](https://github.com/BayRai/ant0-lab/tree/main/articles) — technical write-ups, planned twice a month.

**Principles** — no hype, no performance claims, no screenshots of P&L. Negative results count. If it is not
verified, it is not done.
